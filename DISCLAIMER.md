# Disclaimer

This skill is for defending software you own or are explicitly authorized to
test. Do not use it to scan, attack or analyze third-party systems.

Honeytokens described here are passive: they report when used and do nothing
else. Do not modify them into anything that executes code, manipulates other
software or agents, or retaliates.

The skill helps an AI agent find common problems; it does not guarantee that an
application is secure. Findings can be wrong or incomplete. Review fixes before
applying them, and get a professional assessment for systems that handle
payments, health or other sensitive data.

`examples/vulnerable-vibe-app` is intentionally insecure. Never deploy it. Its
credentials are fake.
